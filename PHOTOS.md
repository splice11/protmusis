# Photos to collect

Download these, name them exactly as below, and put them in `photos/`.
Prefer landscape orientation, at least 1200 px wide. Once they're in the
repo, the slides can be updated to use them in place of the illustrations.

**Good sources:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) (check
the licence on each file page), the [EC Audiovisual Service](https://audiovisual.ec.europa.eu)
(free for EU-related use), the [Council newsroom](https://newsroom.consilium.europa.eu),
and the [ECB banknote pages](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/banknotes).
